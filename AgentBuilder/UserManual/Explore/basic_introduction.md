The Explore section provides a variety of ready-to-use template applications, covering areas such as human resources, assistants, translation, programming, and writing.

## How to Use Template Applications

- Browse the available templates in the Explore section.
- To add a template to your workspace:
  - Click the **Add to Workspace** button on the template card.
  - The application will appear in your workspace on the left side.
- To customize a template and create a new application:
  - Click the **Customize** button on the template.

![Explore Templates](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1727244010107732683.png)


## Workspace Overview

- The workspace serves as your main navigation for applications.
- Click any application in the workspace to use it directly.
- Your workspace includes:
  - Applications you have created
  - Applications added by other team members
