AgentBuilder allows you to quickly publish your AI application as a web app for users. The app will orchestrate work based on your prompt.

---

## Publishing an AI Website
- In the Monitoring page of your application, find the AI website (WebApp) card.
- Enable access to the WebApp to receive a shareable link for users.
- ![WebApp Card](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1723623079198383049.png)

## Setting Up Your AI Site
- Click the **Settings** button on the WebApp card to configure options for your AI website. These settings are visible to end users.
- ![WebApp Settings](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1723623090271737829.png)
