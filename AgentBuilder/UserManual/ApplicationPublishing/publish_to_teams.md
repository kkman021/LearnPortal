You can publish your AgentBuilder application to Microsoft Teams. Once published, users can chat with your bot in Teams.

---

## Preparation
- Microsoft Teams work or school account
- A computer with Microsoft Teams installed

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Create a Bot Service
- Log in to the [Microsoft Azure portal](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/get-started/azure-portal).
- On the **All Services** page, find **AI + machine learning**, then **Bot Services**, and click **View**.
  - ![Bot Services](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1744697579394326046.png)
- Click **Create bot service**.
  - ![Create Bot Service](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1744701505774895384.png)
- Find **Azure Bot** in the list (click **Load more** if needed).
  - ![Azure Bot List](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1744701994310417011.png)
- Click **Create**, fill in the required information, and click **Review + Create**.
  - ![Bot Info](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1744702860027116619.png)
  - ![Review and Create](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1744703022595842634.png)
