Conversation applications engage users in continuous Q&A dialogue. Supported features (enable these during orchestration):
- Variables filled before conversation
- Create, pin, and delete conversations
- Conversation opening statements
- Next-step question suggestions
- Speech-to-text
- References and attributions

---

## Variables Filled Before Conversation
- If variable filling is required, you must provide the prompted information before entering the chat window.
- ![Fill Variables](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1727345728328353984.png)
- Fill in the details and click **Start Conversation** to begin. Hover over AI responses to copy content or provide like/dislike feedback.
- ![Conversation Feedback](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1727345746943534043.png)

## Create, Pin, and Delete Conversations
- Click **New Conversation** to start a new chat. Hover over a conversation to pin or delete it.
- ![Manage Conversations](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1727345757832290981.png)

## Conversation Opener
- If enabled, the AI will automatically start the first line of dialogue in a new conversation.
- ![Conversation Opener](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1727346016528729181.png)

## Follow Up
- If enabled, the system will generate 3 relevant question suggestions after the conversation.
- ![Follow Up](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1727346024522615926.png)
