The application toolkit provides various additional features to enhance your AgentBuilder applications. You can access these toolkits in the Application Orchestration section.

![Application Toolkits](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1737538411391191598.png)

---

## Conversation Opener
- The AI proactively greets or asks a question at the start of a conversation.
- You can edit the opening content to guide users, explain the app background, or lower the barrier for starting a chat.
- ![Conversation Opener](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1725352737055547770.png)

## Follow-up
- The AI generates three follow-up questions based on the previous conversation, guiding the next interaction.
- ![Follow-up](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1725352762399447526.png)

## Text to Speech
- Converts AI-generated responses into natural-sounding speech for playback.
- ![Text to Speech](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1725354174043162327.png)

## Speech to Text
- Allows users to record audio, which is automatically converted to text.
- Optionally, the text can be sent automatically after conversion.
- ![Speech to Text](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1725354195749116572.png)

## Citations and Attributions
- When enabled, the LLM cites content from the knowledge base in its responses.
- Citation details (original text, segment number, match score) are shown below the response.
- Supported preview file types include: TXT, MD, MDX, HTM, HTML, XML.
