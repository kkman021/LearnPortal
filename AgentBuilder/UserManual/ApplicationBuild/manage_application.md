## Editing Application Information
- To modify the application name or description, click **Edit info** in the upper left corner of the application. You can update the icon, name, or description.
- ![Edit Application Info](https://docs.wise-paas.advantech.com/dataSource/resource/1727338482239953864.png)

## Duplicating Applications
- All applications can be copied. Click **Duplicate** in the upper left corner.

## Exporting Applications
- Applications can be exported in DSL format and imported into any AgentBuilder workspace.

## Deleting Applications
- To remove an application, click **Delete** in the upper left corner.
- **Note:** Deletion is permanent. All users lose access, and all prompts, orchestration settings, and logs are deleted.
