## What is EdgeHub?

EdgeHub, with intelligent connectivity at its core, provides comprehensive device management services for Advantech edge devices, enhancing IoT device operational efficiency across various application scenarios. In the industrial IoT market, EdgeHub serves as a unique specification for Advantech hardware, offering differentiated advantages. Through advanced device management capabilities, it helps customers achieve efficient device operations and maintenance.

---

## Why Do Customers Need Hardware with EdgeHub Specifications?

Like any industrial equipment, today’s IoT devices require proactive care to deliver their full value. Even premium hardware can experience degraded functionality or unexpected downtime without proper monitoring and timely updates. This is where intelligent IoT device management becomes essential. EdgeHub’s innovation lies in its device connectivity and management value proposition, giving hardware new specifications that enable secure remote operations and maintenance capabilities, better adapting to customers’ diverse requirements and application scenarios.

---

## Key Features

- **Telemetry Data Collection**  
  Reliably collect and store telemetry data, surviving network and hardware failures. Access collected data via customizable web dashboards or APIs.

- **Multi-Tenancy**  
  Supports multi-tenant and group management. A single tenant may have multiple tenant administrators and millions of devices and customers.

- **Data Visualization**  
  Provides 20+ configurable dashboards and the ability to create custom widgets using a built-in editor.

- **IoT Rule Engine**  
  Processes incoming device data with flexible rule chains based on settings. Forwards data to external systems or triggers alarms using custom settings. Supports complex notification chains and highly customizable rules.

- **Device Management**  
  Enables device registration and management, including monitoring and provisioning. Provides APIs to send commands to devices and vice versa.

- **Alarms and Notification Management**  
  Allows creation and management of alarms related to devices, objects, customers, etc. Supports real-time alarm monitoring and notifications to related entity hierarchies. Raises alarms on device disconnect or inactivity events.

- **Microservices or Monolithic Architecture**  
  Supports monolithic deployment for getting started or small environments, with the ability to upgrade to microservices for high availability and horizontal scalability.

- **SQL, NoSQL, and Hybrid Database Support**  
  Supports both SQL and NoSQL database options, allowing you to choose where to store main entities and data.

- **OTA Update**  
  Allows over-the-air (OTA) firmware/software updates. Tenant administrators can upload packages to the OTA repository and assign them to device profiles or devices. Scheduled OTA updates are supported to avoid downtime.

- **Instant Onboarding**  
  Enables fast, secure, and reliable wireless connectivity deployment in just a few minutes. Administrators can manage Instant On networks from anywhere and ensure that the latest security intelligence protects their networks around the clock.
