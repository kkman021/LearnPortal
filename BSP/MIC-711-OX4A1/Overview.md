## Overview
NVIDIA® Jetson Orin™ NX 16G Lite AI System

- Fanless and ultra-compact design
- Embedded with NVIDIA® Jetson Orin™ NX up to 157 TOPS
- Support 1 x GbE, 2 x USB3.2 Gen1 (5Gbit/s)
- Support 1 x mPCIe, 1 x M.​2 3052, 1 x M.2 2280
- Supports Allxon 24/7 remote monitoring and OTA deployment
- Supports Super mode with Jetpack 6.​2 and above
